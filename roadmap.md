# Emergence roadmap

## Operational core

- Persistent 3D arena editor
- Deterministic agent simulation
- Explorer, collector, survivor, and core-seeker brains
- Live cognition and telemetry
- Replay timeline and speed controls
- Evolution batches and recorded scores
- Public spectator links and global leaderboard

## Multiplayer expansion

- Presence and live cursors in the arena editor
- Human commanders issuing limited high-level orders
- Team lobbies and synchronized match clocks
- Cooperative and competitive objectives
- Tournament seasons and replay sharing

## Intelligence expansion

- User-defined reward functions
- Behavior graphs and model-backed high-level planners
- Agent memory visualization
- Generated arenas from natural-language prompts
- Benchmarks for navigation, cooperation, and resource strategy

## Creator ecosystem

- Reusable arena templates and agent presets
- Published worlds and remix attribution
- Custom assets, rules, and game modes
- Community challenges and featured experiments
